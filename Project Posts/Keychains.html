Student organization commission · Multi-material printing

ESC Keychains & Tokens

Delivered graduation cord charms, then extended the design into personalized leadership tokens.

Problem

The Engineering Student Council requested graduation cord charms featuring its logo.

My contribution

I reconstructed the logo from a reference image and designed parts for multi-material printing using a Bambu Lab AMS. I worked through color separation, print time, and material-use considerations as part of preparing the design for fabrication.

Added value

After completing the charms, I reused the logo to create souvenir tokens for the leadership team, adding each person’s role and service year on the reverse side. This extended the original request into a second personalized product.

Result

Produced physical charms and leadership tokens for the council while developing experience with multi-material design and manufacturing.
