Custom Poker Chips & Golf Markers

Developed customized chips as golf ball markers and souvenirs for school fundraising.

Opportunity

Drawing on my high-school golf experience, I explored a customizable product that teams could use as ball markers or sell as fundraising souvenirs.

My contribution

I modeled the chips in SolidWorks and imported school logos using SVG/vector files. This replaced manual logo reconstruction with a repeatable import workflow for adapting designs to different schools.

Result

Created physical prototypes and discussed the concept with high-school golf coaches in Ohio. The project combines CAD, customization, and exploration of a practical market for a manufactured product.
