About Me

I’m a mechanical engineering student and Prototype Engineer who enjoys turning ideas into parts you can build, test, and improve.

Born and raised in Akron, Ohio, I’ve been curious about how things work since middle school. At Additive Engineering Solutions, I work with large-format additive manufacturing, experimental builds, and materials validation. One of my favorite challenges has been developing a small-scale CNC workflow for hundreds of test samples—finding practical ways to improve machining quality and efficiency while reducing internal costs.

I bring that same problem-solving mindset to student leadership. As President of NAESC, I help lead an organization representing engineering students across more than 40 universities. My work has included helping deliver 12 campus conferences and, during two years as Vice President of Finance, turning losses into net profit through cost reductions, increased billables, and bundled expenses.

Along the way, I enjoy helping others bring their ideas to life through 3D modeling and printing. Outside of engineering, you’ll usually find me on the golf course, exploring the outdoors, or trying a new tool—with another project already in mind.
